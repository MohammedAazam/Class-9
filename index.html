Why This Summit

Education is changing faster than our classrooms are.

AI, automation and shifting job markets are redefining what young people will need to thrive by 2030 — and most schools are being asked to prepare for that future without a shared platform to think it through.

EduAKI Tech X 2030 is AKI Group of Institutions' answer to that gap: a forward-looking summit dedicated to exploring how education transforms through artificial intelligence, emerging technology, future skills, leadership and values-based learning.

It is built as a genuine dialogue, not a lecture series — distinguished educationists, school leaders, technology experts, industry voices, parents and young learners sit on the same platform to deliberate on what today's students actually need.
